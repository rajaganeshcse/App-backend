Privacy Policy for DailyKash

Last updated: September 23, 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Developer Policies

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to DailyKash ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide a mobile rewards and gaming platform that allows registered users to participate in casual activities, spins, scratch cards, offer tasks, and redeem rewards.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application (DailyKash) and associated online services. By installing or using DailyKash, you signify your assent to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services, track legitimate earnings, and protect our ecosystem from abuse, we collect:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, profile avatar URL, and Google account identifier via Google Sign-In.
  • Device Identifiers: Device hardware model, Android OS version, IP address, and installation referrer to prevent fraud, botting, and multi-accounting.
  • Activity & Reward Logs: Coins earned, spin and scratch timestamps, offerwall task completions, referral bonus linkages, and payout transaction records.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process collected data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To calculate, credit, and display your rewards and coin balances.
  • To verify and process payout requests (UPI, Google Play codes, Amazon vouchers, etc.).
  • To detect, prevent, and remediate fraud, unauthorized botting, and VPN abuse.
Privacy Promise: We never sell, rent, or lease your personal information or email to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We integrate industry-standard SDKs that comply with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore cloud database, and Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Compliant rewarded, banner, and interstitial ads.
  • Google Play Services: Account authentication and app integrity checks.
  • Partner Offerwalls: Independent verification of app installs and survey completions.

5. Data Security & Retention

All data is transmitted securely over HTTPS using SSL/TLS 1.2+ encryption. We retain your information as long as your account remains active.

6. Account Deletion & Data Erasure (Google Play Requirement)

Every DailyKash user has the unequivocal right to request complete account deletion and data erasure. You can request deletion at any time via:

  • In-App: Open DailyKash → Drawer Menu → Profile → Delete Account.
  • Web Portal: Submit an instant deletion request on our public web portal.
